Early Warning i
Financing conditions and recession risk. Insolvency is a liquidity event, so these track whether credit is available, not just whether demand is strong.
Sentiment vs Activity — Divergence Check i
Four matched pairs. In each, the same subject is measured twice: once by asking people, once by counting what happened
Standard deviations from each series' own ten-year average, so the two sides are comparable. Both are oriented so that higher means stronger
US · EU · DEEarly WarningEurostat + FREDz-score, 10y
Stated opinion — what people said in a survey. Business confidence, loan officers' own judgement, the Philadelphia Fed's new-orders balance. Moves early, and moves on mood as well as fact.
Recorded activity — what was actually counted. Industrial output, orders booked at Census, loans on bank balance sheets. Slower to arrive, but it happened.
Reading the bars — each is standard deviations from that series' own ten-year average, so a longer bar means further from normal, not a bigger number. Both point the same way: right is stronger. When the yellow bar reaches further right than the green one, confidence has run ahead of what firms are doing. When green leads, the recovery is real but not yet believed.
